# Copyright, Trademarks & Content Provenance

## Summary

PSYSDD encodes the *logic* of psychiatric differential diagnosis — how symptoms cluster, which conditions compete, and the order in which etiologies are ruled out — in **original text written for this project**. It deliberately does **not** reproduce the wording or structure of DSM diagnostic criteria.

## The distinction we rely on

- **Ideas, facts, and systems are not protected by copyright.** The differential-diagnosis *method* (e.g., "rule out substances and medical causes before a primary disorder"), the decision-tree *pathways*, the *relationships* between disorders, and factual thresholds (symptom counts, durations, age cutoffs) are ideas and facts. These may be freely restated.
- **The specific expression is protected.** The exact wording and the particular arrangement of the DSM-5 / DSM-5-TR criteria sets, and the prose of the DSM-5 Handbook of Differential Diagnosis, are copyrighted by the American Psychiatric Association / American Psychiatric Publishing.

## What this means for this repository

1. Every explanatory field — framework prose, decision-point questions, and differential distinctions — is an **original synthesis in the authors' own clinical language.**
2. **No DSM criteria text is reproduced.** Where a user needs the exact criteria (counts, durations, severity thresholds, exclusions), the tool cites them ("per DSM-5-TR criteria for …") and directs the clinician to the official DSM-5-TR text rather than restating it.
3. Diagnostic category **names** are used nominatively, as the standard clinical terminology required to refer to the disorders — not as a claim of authorship.

If you believe any content here reproduces protected expression rather than restating uncopyrightable logic, please open an issue or contact the maintainers; we will review and revise or remove it promptly.
