# 3.1.1 — Intellectual Disability (Intellectual Developmental Disorder)

**Intellectual Disability:** Deficits in intellectual and adaptive functioning with onset during the developmental period.

| Consider and rule out | How it is distinguished from Intellectual Disability |
| --- | --- |
| [Major/Mild Neurocognitive Disorder](3-16-2.md) | Involves acquired decline from a previously attained level of functioning, rather than deficits present from the developmental period. |
| [Specific Learning Disorder](3-1-5.md) | Deficits are circumscribed to specific academic skills without global impairment of intellectual and adaptive functioning. |
| [Communication Disorders / Language Disorder](3-1-2.md) | Impairment is limited to communication/language; nonverbal intellectual and adaptive functioning are relatively preserved. |
| [Autism Spectrum Disorder](3-1-3.md) | Defined by social-communication deficits and restricted/repetitive behavior; may co-occur with intellectual disability, in which case both are diagnosed. |

---
_Distinctions summarize differentiating features only. Confirm the full DSM-5 criteria for each disorder considered._
