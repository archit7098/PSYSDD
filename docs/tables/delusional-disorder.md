# 3.2.3 — Delusional Disorder

**Delusional Disorder:** One or more delusions for ≥1 month without other active-phase schizophrenia symptoms and with relatively preserved functioning apart from the delusion's impact.

| Consider and rule out | How it is distinguished from Delusional Disorder |
| --- | --- |
| [Schizophrenia](3-2-1.md) | Prominent hallucinations, disorganized speech, or negative symptoms are present in addition to delusions. |
| [Obsessive-Compulsive Disorder (with absent insight)](3-6-1.md) | Beliefs are tied to obsessions/compulsions and were once recognized as excessive. |
| [Body Dysmorphic Disorder (with absent insight)](3-6-2.md) | The fixed belief concerns a perceived appearance defect. |
| [Depressive/Bipolar Disorder With Psychotic Features](3-4-1.md) | Delusions occur only during mood episodes. |
| [Paranoid Personality Disorder](3-17-1.md) | Pervasive suspiciousness without a fixed, circumscribed delusion. |
| Substance-Induced or Medical psychotic disorder | Delusions are attributable to a substance or medical condition. |
