# 3.5.6 — Agoraphobia

**Agoraphobia:** Marked fear/avoidance of ≥2 situations (public transport, open/enclosed spaces, crowds, being outside alone) due to fear that escape or help may be unavailable.

| Consider and rule out | How it is distinguished from Agoraphobia |
| --- | --- |
| [Specific Phobia, situational](3-5-3.md) | Fear involves a single situation rather than the ≥2 agoraphobic clusters. |
| [Social Anxiety Disorder](3-5-4.md) | Avoidance is due to fear of scrutiny. |
| [Separation Anxiety Disorder](3-5-1.md) | Avoidance relates to separation from attachment figures. |
| [PTSD](3-7-1.md) | Avoidance is of trauma reminders. |
| [Major Depressive Disorder](3-4-1.md) | Reduced going out reflects anhedonia/amotivation rather than fear of escape difficulty. |

---
_Distinctions summarize differentiating features only. Confirm the full DSM-5 criteria for each disorder considered._
