# 3.5.4 — Social Anxiety Disorder (Social Phobia)

**Social Anxiety Disorder:** Marked fear/anxiety about social situations involving possible scrutiny, with fear of negative evaluation.

| Consider and rule out | How it is distinguished from Social Anxiety Disorder |
| --- | --- |
| [Panic Disorder](3-5-5.md) | Unexpected panic attacks not limited to social situations. |
| [Agoraphobia](3-5-6.md) | Fear is of escape difficulty/incapacitation rather than scrutiny. |
| [Generalized Anxiety Disorder](3-5-7.md) | Worry extends to nonsocial domains and ongoing relationships. |
| [Separation Anxiety Disorder](3-5-1.md) | Discomfort persists even with attachment figures present in social settings. |
| [Autism Spectrum Disorder](3-1-3.md) | Social-communication deficits with lack of age-appropriate relationships; social anxiety patients retain social capacity. |
| [Body Dysmorphic Disorder](3-6-2.md) | Social avoidance stems from beliefs about appearance flaws. |
| [Avoidant Personality Disorder](3-17-8.md) | Pervasive; frequently overlaps and both may be diagnosed. |
| Normal performance anxiety / shyness | Not markedly impairing or distressing. |

---
_Distinctions summarize differentiating features only. Confirm the full DSM-5 criteria for each disorder considered._
