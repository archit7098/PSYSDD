# 3.17.10 — Obsessive-Compulsive Personality Disorder

**Obsessive-Compulsive Personality Disorder:** A pervasive preoccupation with orderliness, perfectionism, and mental/interpersonal control at the expense of flexibility and efficiency.

| Consider and rule out | How it is distinguished from Obsessive-Compulsive Personality Disorder |
| --- | --- |
| [Obsessive-Compulsive Disorder](3-6-1.md) | Presence of true obsessions and compulsions, whereas OCPD involves ego-syntonic traits without them. |
| [Hoarding Disorder](3-6-3.md) | Clinically significant hoarding may warrant a separate diagnosis. |
| [Narcissistic Personality Disorder](3-17-7.md) | Perfectionism serves self-image of superiority rather than orderliness/control for its own sake. |
| [Personality change due to another medical condition](3-17-11.md) | Traits are an acquired change attributable to a medical condition. |
