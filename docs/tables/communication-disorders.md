# 3.1.2 — Communication Disorders

**Communication Disorders (Language, Speech Sound, Childhood-Onset Fluency, Social Pragmatic):** Persistent difficulties in language, speech production, fluency, or the social use of communication.

| Consider and rule out | How it is distinguished from Communication Disorders (Language, Speech Sound, Childhood-Onset Fluency, Social Pragmatic) |
| --- | --- |
| [Intellectual Disability](3-1-1.md) | Communication problems are part of a broader deficit in intellectual and adaptive functioning rather than a circumscribed language/speech deficit. |
| [Autism Spectrum Disorder](3-1-3.md) | Includes restricted/repetitive behaviors and interests in addition to social-communication deficits; Social (Pragmatic) Communication Disorder is diagnosed only when ASD is excluded. |
| Hearing or other sensory impairment | Communication difficulty is fully attributable to a sensory deficit and resolves/adapts with appropriate accommodation. |
| [Selective Mutism](3-5-2.md) | Language capacity is intact but the child fails to speak in specific social situations. |
| Normal variation | Language/speech is within the expected range for developmental level and cultural/linguistic context. |
