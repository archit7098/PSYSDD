# 3.5.5 — Panic Disorder

**Panic Disorder:** Recurrent unexpected panic attacks with ≥1 month of concern about further attacks or maladaptive behavior change.

| Consider and rule out | How it is distinguished from Panic Disorder |
| --- | --- |
| [Other anxiety disorders with cued attacks](3-5-4.md) | Panic attacks are expected (cued by phobic, social, or trauma stimuli) rather than unexpected. |
| Anxiety Disorder Due to Another Medical Condition | Panic is caused by a medical condition (e.g., arrhythmia, hyperthyroidism, pheochromocytoma). |
| Substance/Medication-Induced Anxiety Disorder | Panic attributable to a substance (e.g., caffeine, stimulants) or withdrawal. |
| [Illness Anxiety Disorder](3-9-2.md) | Preoccupation with having a serious illness without recurrent unexpected panic. |
| [Agoraphobia](3-5-6.md) | May co-occur; agoraphobia is coded separately when present. |
