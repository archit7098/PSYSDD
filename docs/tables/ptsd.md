# 3.7.1 — Posttraumatic Stress Disorder or Acute Stress Disorder

**PTSD / Acute Stress Disorder:** Intrusion, avoidance, negative cognition/mood alterations, and arousal following exposure to actual/threatened death, serious injury, or sexual violence (>1 month = PTSD; 3 days–1 month = ASD).

| Consider and rule out | How it is distinguished from PTSD / Acute Stress Disorder |
| --- | --- |
| [Adjustment Disorder](3-7-2.md) | Stressor is not of the required traumatic type, or the response does not meet the PTSD symptom pattern. |
| [Anxiety Disorders / OCD](3-5-7.md) | Intrusive thoughts are not tied to a traumatic event. |
| [Major Depressive Disorder](3-4-1.md) | Lacks the intrusion and trauma-related avoidance symptoms. |
| [Dissociative Disorders](3-8-2.md) | Dissociation occurs without the full PTSD symptom picture. |
| [Psychotic Disorders](3-2-1.md) | Flashbacks are trauma-linked reexperiencing, not primary hallucinations/delusions. |
| Traumatic brain injury | Neurocognitive symptoms are due to the physical injury rather than the psychological trauma. |
