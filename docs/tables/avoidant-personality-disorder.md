# 3.17.8 — Avoidant Personality Disorder

**Avoidant Personality Disorder:** A pervasive pattern of social inhibition, feelings of inadequacy, and hypersensitivity to negative evaluation.

| Consider and rule out | How it is distinguished from Avoidant Personality Disorder |
| --- | --- |
| [Social Anxiety Disorder](3-5-4.md) | Largely overlapping; both may be diagnosed. Personality disorder is broader and earlier-onset. |
| [Schizoid Personality Disorder](3-17-2.md) | Detachment reflects lack of desire for relationships, not fear of rejection. |
| [Dependent Personality Disorder](3-17-9.md) | Focus is on being cared for rather than on avoiding humiliation. |
| [Agoraphobia](3-5-6.md) | Avoidance is of situations where escape is difficult rather than of social scrutiny. |

---
_Distinctions summarize differentiating features only. Confirm the full DSM-5 criteria for each disorder considered._
