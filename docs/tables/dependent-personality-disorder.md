# 3.17.9 — Dependent Personality Disorder

**Dependent Personality Disorder:** A pervasive and excessive need to be taken care of, leading to submissive/clinging behavior and fears of separation.

| Consider and rule out | How it is distinguished from Dependent Personality Disorder |
| --- | --- |
| [Borderline Personality Disorder](3-17-5.md) | Reacts to abandonment with rage/emptiness rather than submission and seeking a new caretaker. |
| [Histrionic Personality Disorder](3-17-6.md) | Uses gregarious, demanding attention seeking rather than submissive dependence. |
| [Avoidant Personality Disorder](3-17-8.md) | Withdraws from relationships until certain of acceptance, rather than clinging. |
| Dependence due to a medical condition / mood disorder | Dependent behavior is secondary to another disorder. |
