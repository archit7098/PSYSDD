# 3.17.2 — Schizoid Personality Disorder

**Schizoid Personality Disorder:** Pervasive detachment from social relationships and a restricted range of emotional expression.

| Consider and rule out | How it is distinguished from Schizoid Personality Disorder |
| --- | --- |
| [Schizotypal Personality Disorder](3-17-3.md) | Adds cognitive-perceptual distortions and eccentric behavior. |
| [Avoidant Personality Disorder](3-17-8.md) | Social withdrawal stems from fear of rejection, with a desire for closeness. |
| [Autism Spectrum Disorder](3-1-3.md) | Includes restricted/repetitive behaviors and early developmental social-communication deficits. |
| [Schizophrenia (residual/negative)](3-2-1.md) | History of active-phase psychotic symptoms. |
| [Major Depressive Disorder](3-4-1.md) | Social withdrawal is episodic and mood-linked. |
