# 3.5.7 — Generalized Anxiety Disorder

**Generalized Anxiety Disorder:** Excessive, hard-to-control worry about multiple domains, more days than not for ≥6 months, with somatic symptoms.

| Consider and rule out | How it is distinguished from Generalized Anxiety Disorder |
| --- | --- |
| Anxiety Disorder Due to Another Medical Condition / Substance-Induced | Anxiety is attributable to a medical condition or substance. |
| [Social Anxiety / Panic / Specific Phobia](3-5-4.md) | Worry is confined to a specific focus rather than generalized. |
| [Obsessive-Compulsive Disorder](3-6-1.md) | Anxiety is driven by intrusive obsessions rather than realistic-themed worry. |
| [Illness Anxiety Disorder](3-9-2.md) | Worry centers specifically on having a serious illness. |
| [Major Depressive Disorder / Adjustment Disorder](3-4-1.md) | Worry is confined to a depressive episode or a stressor-related adjustment reaction. |
| Normal worry | Worry is controllable, not markedly distressing, and not accompanied by the somatic symptom burden. |
