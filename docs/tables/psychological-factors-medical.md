# 3.9.4 — Psychological Factors Affecting Other Medical Conditions

**Psychological Factors Affecting Other Medical Conditions:** Psychological/behavioral factors that adversely affect a documented medical condition by influencing its course, treatment, or pathophysiology.

| Consider and rule out | How it is distinguished from Psychological Factors Affecting Other Medical Conditions |
| --- | --- |
| Mental disorder with a medical cause | There a psychiatric disorder is caused by the medical condition, not psychological factors worsening it. |
| [Adjustment Disorder](3-7-2.md) | A psychological response to the medical illness that itself meets disorder criteria, rather than factors worsening the illness. |
| [Somatic Symptom Disorder](3-9-1.md) | Excessive thoughts/behaviors about symptoms dominate rather than measurable worsening of a medical condition. |
| [Illness Anxiety Disorder](3-9-2.md) | Health anxiety predominates without a medical condition being worsened. |
