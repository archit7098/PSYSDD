# 3.5.1 — Separation Anxiety Disorder

**Separation Anxiety Disorder:** Developmentally inappropriate, excessive fear/anxiety about separation from attachment figures.

| Consider and rule out | How it is distinguished from Separation Anxiety Disorder |
| --- | --- |
| [Generalized Anxiety Disorder](3-5-7.md) | Worry spans many domains rather than focusing on separation. |
| [Panic Disorder / Agoraphobia](3-5-5.md) | Fear centers on panic symptoms or escape difficulty rather than separation from attachment figures. |
| [Social Anxiety Disorder](3-5-4.md) | Avoidance is due to fear of scrutiny, not separation. |
| [Autism Spectrum Disorder](3-1-3.md) | School/social refusal relates to sensory/social intolerance rather than separation fear. |
| [Conduct Disorder / Truancy](3-14-3.md) | School avoidance is to pursue activities outside home rather than to stay near attachment figures. |
| [Illness Anxiety / PTSD](3-7-1.md) | Fear is focused on illness or trauma reminders rather than separation. |
