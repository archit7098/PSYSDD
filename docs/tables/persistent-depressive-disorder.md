# 3.4.2 — Persistent Depressive Disorder (Dysthymia)

**Persistent Depressive Disorder:** Depressed mood most of the day, more days than not, for ≥2 years (≥1 year in youth), with additional depressive symptoms and no symptom-free interval longer than 2 months.

| Consider and rule out | How it is distinguished from Persistent Depressive Disorder |
| --- | --- |
| [Major Depressive Disorder](3-4-1.md) | Episodic rather than chronic; MDD and persistent depressive disorder may be diagnosed together when criteria for both are met. |
| [Bipolar Disorders / Cyclothymic Disorder](3-3-3.md) | History of hypomanic/manic symptoms. |
| [Psychotic disorders (residual)](3-2-1.md) | Chronic low mood occurs within a primary psychotic illness. |
| Depressive Disorder Due to Another Medical Condition / Substance-Induced | Chronic depression is attributable to a medical condition or substance. |
| Personality Disorders | Enduring maladaptive traits without pervasive depressed mood (may co-occur). |
