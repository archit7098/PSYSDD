# 3.14.2 — Intermittent Explosive Disorder

**Intermittent Explosive Disorder:** Recurrent impulsive/anger-based aggressive outbursts grossly out of proportion to provocation, not premeditated.

| Consider and rule out | How it is distinguished from Intermittent Explosive Disorder |
| --- | --- |
| [Conduct Disorder / Antisocial Personality Disorder](3-14-3.md) | Aggression is instrumental/premeditated and part of a broader antisocial pattern. |
| [Disruptive Mood Dysregulation Disorder](3-4-4.md) | Persistent irritable mood between outbursts; DMDD takes precedence in youth when both are met. |
| [Bipolar Disorder](3-3-1.md) | Outbursts occur during manic episodes. |
| [Substance Intoxication/Withdrawal](3-15-1.md) | Aggression occurs in the context of substance use. |
| [Personality Disorders (borderline, antisocial)](3-17-5.md) | Aggression is part of a pervasive personality pattern. |
| Aggression due to another medical condition | Outbursts are the physiological consequence of a medical condition (e.g., head trauma). |

---
_Distinctions summarize differentiating features only. Confirm the full DSM-5 criteria for each disorder considered._
