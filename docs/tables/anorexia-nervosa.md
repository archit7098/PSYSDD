# 3.10.2 — Anorexia Nervosa

**Anorexia Nervosa:** Restriction of intake leading to significantly low weight, intense fear of weight gain, and disturbance in experience of body weight/shape.

| Consider and rule out | How it is distinguished from Anorexia Nervosa |
| --- | --- |
| [Bulimia Nervosa](3-10-3.md) | Body weight is maintained at or above normal despite binge-purge cycles. |
| [Avoidant/Restrictive Food Intake Disorder](3-10-1.md) | Low weight without body-image disturbance or fear of weight gain. |
| Medical causes of low weight | Weight loss is due to a medical illness without the characteristic body-image disturbance. |
| [Major Depressive Disorder](3-4-1.md) | Weight loss from appetite reduction without fear of weight gain or body-image disturbance. |
| [Body Dysmorphic Disorder](3-6-2.md) | Preoccupation concerns appearance features other than weight/shape. |
| [Psychotic disorders](3-2-1.md) | Food refusal is due to delusions (e.g., of poisoning) rather than weight concern. |
