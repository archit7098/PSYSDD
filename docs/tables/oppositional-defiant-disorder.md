# 3.14.1 — Oppositional Defiant Disorder

**Oppositional Defiant Disorder:** A pattern of angry/irritable mood, argumentative/defiant behavior, or vindictiveness lasting ≥6 months.

| Consider and rule out | How it is distinguished from Oppositional Defiant Disorder |
| --- | --- |
| [Conduct Disorder](3-14-3.md) | Involves violation of others' basic rights and major societal norms, beyond defiance. |
| [Disruptive Mood Dysregulation Disorder](3-4-4.md) | Severe temper outbursts plus persistent irritability; when both are met, DMDD is diagnosed instead. |
| [ADHD](3-1-4.md) | Noncompliance stems from inattention/impulsivity rather than deliberate defiance (often co-occurs). |
| [Depressive/Bipolar Disorders](3-4-1.md) | Oppositional behavior occurs only during mood episodes. |
| [Intermittent Explosive Disorder](3-14-2.md) | Characterized by serious aggressive outbursts rather than a broad defiant pattern. |
| Normal developmental oppositionality | Behavior is within expectable frequency/intensity for developmental stage. |
