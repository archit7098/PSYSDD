# 3.2.4 — Brief Psychotic Disorder

**Brief Psychotic Disorder:** Sudden onset of ≥1 positive psychotic symptom lasting at least 1 day but less than 1 month, with eventual full return to premorbid functioning.

| Consider and rule out | How it is distinguished from Brief Psychotic Disorder |
| --- | --- |
| [Schizophreniform Disorder / Schizophrenia](3-2-1.md) | Symptoms persist for 1 month or longer. |
| [Mood Disorder With Psychotic Features](3-4-1.md) | Psychosis occurs in the context of a manic or major depressive episode. |
| Substance/Medication-Induced Psychotic Disorder | Psychosis is attributable to substance intoxication/withdrawal or medication. |
| Psychotic Disorder Due to Another Medical Condition | Psychosis is the physiological consequence of a medical condition (including delirium). |
| [Personality Disorders (e.g., borderline, schizotypal)](3-17-5.md) | Transient stress-related paranoia/dissociation without a discrete psychotic episode. |

---
_Distinctions summarize differentiating features only. Confirm the full DSM-5 criteria for each disorder considered._
