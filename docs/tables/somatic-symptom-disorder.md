# 3.9.1 — Somatic Symptom Disorder

**Somatic Symptom Disorder:** One or more distressing somatic symptoms plus excessive thoughts, feelings, or behaviors related to them.

| Consider and rule out | How it is distinguished from Somatic Symptom Disorder |
| --- | --- |
| [Illness Anxiety Disorder](3-9-2.md) | Somatic symptoms are minimal/absent; the focus is fear of having/acquiring illness. |
| Other medical condition | A medical condition explains the symptoms and the psychological response is proportionate. |
| [Panic Disorder](3-5-5.md) | Somatic symptoms are episodic and confined to panic attacks. |
| [Generalized Anxiety Disorder](3-5-7.md) | Worry is generalized rather than focused on somatic symptoms. |
| [Conversion Disorder](3-9-3.md) | Symptoms are specifically neurological and incompatible with recognized disease. |
| [Depressive Disorders](3-4-1.md) | Somatic complaints occur only within a depressive episode. |
| [Body Dysmorphic Disorder](3-6-2.md) | Preoccupation is with appearance rather than somatic symptoms. |

---
_Distinctions summarize differentiating features only. Confirm the full DSM-5 criteria for each disorder considered._
