# 3.6.2 — Body Dysmorphic Disorder

**Body Dysmorphic Disorder:** Preoccupation with perceived defects/flaws in appearance not observable or slight to others, with repetitive behaviors.

| Consider and rule out | How it is distinguished from Body Dysmorphic Disorder |
| --- | --- |
| Normal appearance concerns | Not preoccupying, distressing, or impairing. |
| [Eating Disorders](3-10-2.md) | Concern is specifically about body weight/shape and eating behavior. |
| [Obsessive-Compulsive Disorder](3-6-1.md) | Obsessions/compulsions are not limited to appearance. |
| [Social Anxiety Disorder](3-5-4.md) | Fear of scrutiny is not based on a specific perceived appearance defect. |
| [Major Depressive Disorder](3-4-1.md) | Appearance-related negative thoughts occur only within a depressive episode. |
| [Illness Anxiety Disorder](3-9-2.md) | Preoccupation is with illness rather than appearance. |
