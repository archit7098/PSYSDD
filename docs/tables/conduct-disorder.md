# 3.14.3 — Conduct Disorder

**Conduct Disorder:** A repetitive, persistent pattern of behavior violating others' basic rights or major age-appropriate societal norms.

| Consider and rule out | How it is distinguished from Conduct Disorder |
| --- | --- |
| [Oppositional Defiant Disorder](3-14-1.md) | Defiance and irritability without violation of others' basic rights. |
| [Antisocial Personality Disorder](3-17-4.md) | The adult (≥18) continuation; conduct disorder is required before age 15. |
| [ADHD](3-1-4.md) | Impulsive rule-breaking without a sustained pattern of rights violations (often co-occurs). |
| [Bipolar/Depressive Disorders](3-3-1.md) | Antisocial behavior occurs only during mood episodes. |
| [Intermittent Explosive Disorder](3-14-2.md) | Aggression is impulsive outbursts without the broader pattern of nonaggressive conduct violations. |
| [Adjustment Disorder](3-7-2.md) | Conduct disturbance is a time-limited reaction to a stressor. |

---
_Distinctions summarize differentiating features only. Confirm the full DSM-5 criteria for each disorder considered._
