# 3.12.1 — Sexual Dysfunctions

**Sexual Dysfunctions:** Clinically significant disturbance in sexual response or pleasure, typically ≥6 months, causing distress.

| Consider and rule out | How it is distinguished from Sexual Dysfunctions |
| --- | --- |
| Substance/Medication-Induced Sexual Dysfunction | Dysfunction is attributable to a substance or medication (e.g., SSRIs, antihypertensives). |
| Sexual dysfunction due to another medical condition | Fully explained by a medical condition (vascular, neurologic, endocrine). |
| [Another nonsexual mental disorder](3-4-1.md) | Dysfunction is better explained by another disorder (e.g., major depressive disorder). |
| Severe relationship distress or other stressors | Dysfunction is attributable to relationship problems, partner factors, or contextual stressors. |
| Normal variation / inadequate stimulation | Response is within normal limits given age, stimulation, and context, and is not distressing. |
