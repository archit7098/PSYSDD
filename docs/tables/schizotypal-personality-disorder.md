# 3.17.3 — Schizotypal Personality Disorder

**Schizotypal Personality Disorder:** Pervasive social/interpersonal deficits with acute discomfort in close relationships, plus cognitive-perceptual distortions and eccentric behavior.

| Consider and rule out | How it is distinguished from Schizotypal Personality Disorder |
| --- | --- |
| [Schizophrenia / Delusional Disorder](3-2-1.md) | Involves frank, sustained psychotic symptoms rather than attenuated ones. |
| [Schizoid/Paranoid Personality Disorders](3-17-2.md) | Lack the cognitive-perceptual distortions and eccentricity of schizotypal. |
| [Autism Spectrum Disorder](3-1-3.md) | Social deficits stem from early developmental communication impairment and restricted interests. |
| Psychotic Disorder Due to Substance/Medical | Attenuated psychotic-like features are attributable to a substance or medical condition. |
