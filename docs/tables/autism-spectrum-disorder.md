# 3.1.3 — Autism Spectrum Disorder

**Autism Spectrum Disorder:** Persistent deficits in social communication/interaction plus restricted, repetitive patterns of behavior, interests, or activities, with onset in the early developmental period.

| Consider and rule out | How it is distinguished from Autism Spectrum Disorder |
| --- | --- |
| [Social (Pragmatic) Communication Disorder](3-1-2.md) | Social-communication deficits are present but restricted/repetitive behaviors are absent. |
| [Intellectual Disability](3-1-1.md) | Social communication is commensurate with developmental level; diagnosed additionally when social-communication deficits exceed developmental level. |
| [Attention-Deficit/Hyperactivity Disorder](3-1-4.md) | Attentional/behavioral problems without the qualitative social-communication deficits and restricted interests of ASD (may co-occur). |
| [Selective Mutism](3-5-2.md) | Early development and social communication are typically normal; failure to speak is situation-specific. |
| Stereotypic Movement Disorder | Stereotypies occur without the social-communication deficits of ASD. |
| [Schizophrenia (childhood onset)](3-2-1.md) | Includes a period of delusions/hallucinations and follows a prodrome; ASD lacks positive psychotic symptoms. |
