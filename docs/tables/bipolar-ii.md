# 3.3.2 — Bipolar II Disorder

**Bipolar II Disorder:** At least one Hypomanic Episode and at least one Major Depressive Episode, with no history of a full Manic Episode.

| Consider and rule out | How it is distinguished from Bipolar II Disorder |
| --- | --- |
| [Bipolar I Disorder](3-3-1.md) | A full manic episode has occurred at some point. |
| [Major Depressive Disorder](3-4-1.md) | No lifetime hypomanic episode. |
| [Cyclothymic Disorder](3-3-3.md) | Symptoms never reach full hypomanic or major depressive episode thresholds. |
| [Borderline Personality Disorder](3-17-5.md) | Affective instability is brief and reactive rather than sustained hypomanic episodes. |
| Substance/Medication-Induced Bipolar Disorder | Hypomania is attributable to a substance and does not persist with abstinence. |
