# 3.6.4 — Trichotillomania (Hair-Pulling Disorder)

**Trichotillomania:** Recurrent pulling out of one's hair causing hair loss, with repeated attempts to stop.

| Consider and rule out | How it is distinguished from Trichotillomania |
| --- | --- |
| [Obsessive-Compulsive Disorder](3-6-1.md) | Hair pulling is not performed in response to an obsession. |
| [Body Dysmorphic Disorder](3-6-2.md) | Hair removal is to correct a perceived appearance defect. |
| [Stereotypic Movement Disorder / Tics](3-1-6.md) | Movements are stereotyped or tic-like rather than focused hair pulling. |
| [Psychotic disorder](3-2-1.md) | Pulling responds to a delusion or hallucination. |
| Dermatological/medical hair loss | Alopecia is due to a medical condition rather than pulling. |

---
_Distinctions summarize differentiating features only. Confirm the full DSM-5 criteria for each disorder considered._
