# 3.4.4 — Disruptive Mood Dysregulation Disorder

**Disruptive Mood Dysregulation Disorder:** Severe recurrent temper outbursts with persistently irritable/angry mood between outbursts, onset before age 10, diagnosed between ages 6 and 18.

| Consider and rule out | How it is distinguished from Disruptive Mood Dysregulation Disorder |
| --- | --- |
| [Bipolar Disorder](3-3-1.md) | Irritability is episodic with distinct periods of mood elevation/increased energy, not chronic. |
| [Oppositional Defiant Disorder](3-14-1.md) | DMDD requires severe outbursts and persistent inter-outburst irritability; when both sets of criteria are met, only DMDD is diagnosed. |
| [Intermittent Explosive Disorder](3-14-2.md) | Lacks the persistent negative mood between outbursts that characterizes DMDD. |
| [ADHD / Conduct Disorder](3-1-4.md) | May co-occur; distinguished by the chronic irritable mood core of DMDD. |
| [Major Depressive Disorder / Anxiety Disorders](3-4-1.md) | If full criteria for DMDD and MDD are both met, DMDD is not additionally diagnosed. |

---
_Distinctions summarize differentiating features only. Confirm the full DSM-5 criteria for each disorder considered._
