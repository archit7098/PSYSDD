# 3.9.5 — Factitious Disorder

**Factitious Disorder:** Falsification of physical/psychological signs or symptoms, or induction of injury/disease, in the absence of obvious external rewards.

| Consider and rule out | How it is distinguished from Factitious Disorder |
| --- | --- |
| Malingering | Feigning is motivated by a tangible external incentive (money, drugs, avoiding duties). |
| [Somatic Symptom Disorder](3-9-1.md) | Symptoms are genuinely experienced rather than deliberately falsified. |
| [Conversion Disorder](3-9-3.md) | Symptoms are not intentionally produced. |
| [Borderline Personality Disorder](3-17-5.md) | Self-harm is to relieve distress rather than to deceive and assume the sick role. |
| Genuine medical condition | Signs are authentic and not induced or falsified. |
