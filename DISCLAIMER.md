# Medical & Legal Disclaimer

**PSYSDD is an educational decision-support reference for qualified clinicians and trainees. It is not a medical device, not a diagnostic authority, and not a source of individualized medical advice.**

## Not a substitute for clinical judgment

The decision trees and differential tables in this project summarize *differentiating features* drawn from DSM-5 differential-diagnosis methodology. They deliberately omit full criteria sets, duration and course requirements, severity thresholds, exclusion criteria, and the clinical nuance that a real evaluation requires. Reaching an endpoint in a decision tree does **not** establish that a patient meets criteria for that disorder.

Every use must be accompanied by:

- A complete clinical evaluation (history, collateral, mental status examination, and, where indicated, physical examination and laboratory/imaging workup);
- Review of the **full DSM-5 criteria** for any disorder under consideration; and
- The clinician's own judgment and accumulated experience.

## No diagnosis, no treatment guidance

This tool does not diagnose, does not recommend treatment, and must not be used as the sole basis for any diagnostic, therapeutic, disposition, or risk decision. Suicidality, homicidality, and medical emergencies require immediate assessment and management independent of any tool.
